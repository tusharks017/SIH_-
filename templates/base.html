Privacy Policy
Data Collection & Use

SECURELEX collects only essential data required for authentication and audit trail tracking, including username, upload timestamps, and access logs.

Data Storage

All legal documents are securely stored with role-based access control. Documents marked as admin-only are restricted exclusively to authorized administrators.

Terms of Service
Usage Terms

SECURELEX is intended strictly for authorized legal and investigative document management. Users must maintain confidentiality of their credentials.

Dynamic Watermarking

All downloaded documents contain automated watermarks containing the user's identity to enforce chain-of-custody tracking.

Security Standards
Audit Trails

Every download, preview, upload, and deletion action is immutably recorded in system audit logs.

Password Hashing

User credentials are encrypted using industry-standard scrypt cryptographic hashing algorithms.

This site uses only essential session cookies required for secure login. No tracking cookies are used.
